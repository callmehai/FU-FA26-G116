title UC-61 - Submit Meeting Minutes for Approval

actor "Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Minutes Card (Biên bản tab)" as MinutesCard
control ":Minutes Controller" as MinutesController
control ":Minutes Service" as MinutesService
participant ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->MinutesCard: Confirm Submit for Approval
activate MinutesCard
MinutesCard->MinutesController: submit(minutesId, updatedAt)
activate MinutesController
MinutesController->MinutesService: SubmitAsync(minutesId, actorId, updatedAt)
activate MinutesService
MinutesService->Database: Lock the Meeting and the revision, and retrieve both (FOR UPDATE)
activate Database
Database-->MinutesService: Meeting and revision / None
deactivate Database

alt Meeting not accessible, or actor is neither the current Chair nor the current Secretary - EX-6
    MinutesService-->MinutesController: Not found
    MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 404
    MinutesCard-->Actor: No minutes
else Actor is the current Chair, not the Secretary - EX-1
    MinutesService-->MinutesController: MSG425 - Only the Secretary may submit the minutes for approval.
    MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 403 MSG425
    MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG425 - Only the Secretary may submit the minutes for approval.
else Actor is the current Secretary
    MinutesService->MinutesService: Check the Meeting has Ended (BR-405), the revision is Draft or returned (BR-407)\nand it is the copy the actor read (BR-389)

    alt The Meeting has not ended - EX-2
        MinutesService-->MinutesController: MSG426 - The minutes can be submitted only after the meeting has ended.
        MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 409 MSG426
        MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG426 - The minutes can be submitted only after the meeting has ended.
    else Already submitted or issued - EX-3
        MinutesService-->MinutesController: MSG400 - The minutes have been submitted or issued and can no longer be changed.
        MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 409 MSG400
        MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG400 - The minutes have been submitted or issued and can no longer be changed.
    else Saved since the actor read it - EX-4
        MinutesService-->MinutesController: MSG401 - The minutes were just changed by someone else. Reload to see the latest version.
        MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 409 MSG401
        MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG401 - The minutes were just changed by someone else. Reload to see the latest version.
    else Submission valid
        MinutesService->Database: Set WaitingApproval, who submitted and when, together with\nthe Chair's notification, in one save (BR-388, BR-411)
        activate Database
        Database-->MinutesService: Saved / Failed
        deactivate Database

        alt Saved
            MinutesService->NotificationService: The notification is saved with the state; the dispatcher pushes it
            activate NotificationService
            NotificationService-->MinutesService: Queued
            deactivate NotificationService
            MinutesService-->MinutesController: State
            MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 200 MSG424 - Minutes submitted to the Chair for approval.
            MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG424 - Minutes submitted to the Chair for approval.
        else The change cannot be saved - EX-5
            MinutesService-->MinutesController: MSG404 - Unable to save the minutes. Please try again.
            MinutesController-->MinutesCard: 503 MSG404
            MinutesCard-->Actor: MSG404 - Unable to save the minutes. Please try again.
        end
    end
end
deactivate MinutesService
deactivate MinutesController
deactivate MinutesCard
