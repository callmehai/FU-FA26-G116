title UC-85a - Find Meeting Time Slots

actor "Meeting Organizer" as Finder
boundary "Find Meeting Time Page" as FindPage
control ":Meeting Slot Controller" as SlotController
control ":Meeting Slot Service" as SlotService
participant ":User Repository" as UserRepository
participant ":Calendar Share Repository" as ShareRepository
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

note right of SlotService: The invitee list reuses GET /api/meetings/participant-options (MeetingCreator, active, id/fullName/email).

Finder->FindPage: Find meeting time (invitees, duration, dates, hours, buffer, step)
activate FindPage

FindPage->SlotController: Find meeting time
activate SlotController

SlotController->SlotService: findSlots(finderId, searchInput)
activate SlotService

SlotService->UserRepository: Verify finder is active and holds MeetingCreator (BR-430)
activate UserRepository
UserRepository->Database: Retrieve finder account with capabilities
activate Database
Database-->UserRepository: Account / Not found
deactivate Database
UserRepository-->SlotService: Verification result
deactivate UserRepository

alt Finder has no right to create meetings - EX-2
    SlotService-->SlotController: MSG448 - Only a Meeting Organizer can do this.
    deactivate SlotService
    SlotController-->FindPage: 403 MSG448
    deactivate SlotController
    FindPage-->Finder: MSG448 - Only a Meeting Organizer can do this.
else Finder is verified
    SlotService->SlotService: Validate at most 10 people besides the finder, 15 minutes to 8 hours,\nrange at most 31 days, weekday hours, buffer 0 to 4 hours, step 5 minutes to 2 hours (BR-421, BR-422)

    alt Search parameters are invalid - EX-1
        SlotService-->SlotController: MSG444 - The meeting-time search is invalid. Check the highlighted fields.
        deactivate SlotService
        SlotController-->FindPage: 400 MSG444
        deactivate SlotController
        FindPage-->Finder: MSG444 - The meeting-time search is invalid. Check the highlighted fields:\nToo many people. Duration, range, hours, buffer or step is out of range.
    else Search parameters are valid
        SlotService->ShareRepository: Get Effective shares of each invitee's calendar to the finder (BR-423, BR-424, BR-425)
        activate ShareRepository
        ShareRepository->Database: Retrieve shares with status and end date >= today
        activate Database
        Database-->ShareRepository: Effective shares (stopped, expired and past-end-date shares excluded)
        deactivate Database
        ShareRepository-->SlotService: Effective shares
        deactivate ShareRepository

        SlotService->MeetingRepository: Get meetings of finder and invitees overlapping the range
        activate MeetingRepository
        MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve overlapping meetings with roles and actual end times
        activate Database
        Database-->MeetingRepository: Meetings / Read failed
        deactivate Database
        MeetingRepository-->SlotService: Meetings / Read failed
        deactivate MeetingRepository

        alt Shares or meetings cannot be read - EX-3
            SlotService-->SlotController: MSG453 - Unable to load the calendar sharing data. Please try again.
            deactivate SlotService
            SlotController-->FindPage: 500 MSG453
            deactivate SlotController
            FindPage-->Finder: MSG453 - Unable to load the calendar sharing data. Please try again.
        else Data is loaded
            SlotService->SlotService: Keep only meetings at shared levels (BR-414, BR-417).\nCount the finder's own meetings only when "Tính cả tôi" is ticked,\nthen at every level (BR-416, BR-418).\nCount only current Chair, Secretary or Member assignments as busy;\ndeclined invitations, creator-only and approver-only never count as busy (BR-419).\nCount Chờ duyệt, approved, published, ongoing and ended meetings until their actual end;\nnever Draft, Rejected or cancelled ones (BR-420).\nMark busy with the gap before and after every busy period,\nthen round start times up to the step; skip past start times, keep out-of-range days unknown (BR-415, BR-422)

            alt No slot fits everyone - AF-1
                SlotService-->SlotController: Slots with the most people free and who is busy
                deactivate SlotService
                SlotController-->FindPage: 200 MSG445 - No time slot fits everyone. Showing the slots with the most people free.
                deactivate SlotController
                FindPage-->Finder: MSG445 - No time slot fits everyone. Showing the slots with the most people free.\nUnknown days and people without a share are never counted as free (BR-415, BR-416)
            else Common slots found
                SlotService-->SlotController: Common slots by day
                deactivate SlotService
                SlotController-->FindPage: 200 Common slots by day
                deactivate SlotController
                FindPage-->Finder: Common slots by day.\nOnly shared calendars are counted. Invitees may still be busy (BR-428)
            end
        end
    end
end

deactivate SlotController
deactivate FindPage
