title UC-12 - Update Meeting

actor "Meeting Organizer" as Organizer
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Organizer->MeetingPage: Select meeting and choose Edit Meeting
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->MeetingController: Request current meeting information
activate MeetingController
MeetingController->MeetingService: Get meeting for update
activate MeetingService
MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get current meeting
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Current meeting information
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Current meeting information
MeetingService-->MeetingController: Current meeting information
MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Current meeting information
MeetingPage-->Organizer: Display editable meeting information
Organizer->MeetingPage: Modify editable fields
Organizer->MeetingPage: Submit updated meeting information
MeetingPage->MeetingController: Update meeting
MeetingController->MeetingService: Update meeting
MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get current meeting status
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve current meeting status
Database-->MeetingRepository: Current meeting status
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Current meeting status

alt Meeting is no longer editable - EX-2
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: Meeting is no longer editable
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG03 - This meeting can no longer be edited.
    MeetingPage-->Organizer: Display edit restriction message
else Meeting is editable
    MeetingService->MeetingService: Validate submitted meeting information\n(BR-25, BR-27)
    loop Meeting information is invalid - EX-1
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: Invalid meeting information
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Validation errors
        MeetingPage-->Organizer: Display validation messages
        Organizer->MeetingPage: Correct and resubmit meeting information
        MeetingPage->MeetingController: Update meeting
        MeetingController->MeetingService: Update meeting
        MeetingService->MeetingService: Validate corrected information\n(BR-25, BR-27)
    end
    MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Update meeting information\n(BR-28)
    MeetingRepository->Database: Store updated meeting information
    alt Meeting cannot be updated - EX-3
        Database-->MeetingRepository: Update failed
        MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting update failed
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: Unable to update meeting
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG04 - Unable to update the meeting.\nPlease try again.
        MeetingPage-->Organizer: Display update error
    else Meeting updated successfully
        Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting updated
        MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Updated meeting
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: Meeting updated successfully
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG02 - Meeting updated successfully.
        MeetingPage-->Organizer: Display success message
    end
end
deactivate Database
deactivate MeetingRepository
deactivate MeetingService
deactivate MeetingController
deactivate MeetingPage
