title UC-16 - View Meeting Details

actor "Meeting Organizer / Meeting Approver / Chair / Secretary / Participant" as User
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

User->MeetingPage: Request meeting details
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->MeetingController: View meeting details
activate MeetingController
MeetingController->MeetingService: getMeetingDetails(meetingId, userId)
activate MeetingService
MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get accessible meeting (BR-29)
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting and verify authorized relationship
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Accessible meeting / Not found
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Accessible meeting / Not found
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt Meeting does not exist or is not accessible - EX-1
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: Not found (same result for both cases)
    deactivate MeetingService
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Not found
    deactivate MeetingController
    MeetingPage-->User: Meeting not found or unavailable
else Meeting is accessible
    activate MeetingController
    activate MeetingService
    MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get meeting-level details
    activate MeetingRepository
    MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting-level fields, Planned Start/End Time, Actual Start/End Time\nwhen available and, for a Cancelled Meeting, its cancellation history entry
    activate Database
    Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting details / Retrieval failed
    deactivate Database
    MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting details / Retrieval failed
    deactivate MeetingRepository
    alt Meeting details cannot be retrieved - EX-2
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG37 - Unable to load meeting details. Please try again.
        deactivate MeetingService
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return loading failure
        deactivate MeetingController
        MeetingPage-->User: MSG37 - Unable to load meeting details. Please try again.
    else Meeting details retrieved successfully
        activate MeetingController
        activate MeetingService
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: Meeting Name, Planned Start/End Time, Actual Start/End Time\nwhen available, Mode, Location, Confidentiality Level, Description, Status;\nif Cancelled: cancellation reason, who cancelled it and when (BR-298)
        deactivate MeetingService
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return meeting information with planned and available actual times
        deactivate MeetingController
        MeetingPage-->User: Display meeting-level details and planned/actual times read-only;\nfor a Cancelled Meeting also show reason, actor and time
    end
end

deactivate MeetingPage
