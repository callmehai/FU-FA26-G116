title UC-16 - View Meeting Details

actor "User" as User
boundary "Meeting Details Page" as DetailsPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

User->DetailsPage: Open meeting details
activate DetailsPage

DetailsPage->MeetingController: Get meeting details (meetingId)
activate MeetingController

MeetingController->MeetingService: getMeetingDetails(meetingId, actor)
activate MeetingService

MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Find meeting and check access
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting and access
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting / not found
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting / not found
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt Meeting does not exist or is not accessible - EX-1
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG37 - Meeting details are not accessible
    MeetingController-->DetailsPage: Return not found
    DetailsPage-->User: MSG37 - Meeting details are not accessible

else Meeting accessible
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: Meeting details
    MeetingController-->DetailsPage: Meeting details
    DetailsPage-->User: Show meeting details (BR-298)
end

deactivate MeetingService
deactivate MeetingController
deactivate DetailsPage
EOF
