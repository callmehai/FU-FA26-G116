title UC-20 - Publish Meeting

actor "Meeting Organizer" as Organizer
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
control ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
database ":Database" as Database

Organizer->MeetingPage: Publish meeting
activate MeetingPage

MeetingPage->MeetingController: Publish meeting (meetingId)
activate MeetingController

MeetingController->MeetingService: publishMeeting(meetingId)
activate MeetingService

MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Find meeting and verify eligibility
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting, status, chair/secretary
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and participants
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting and participants
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt Meeting is no longer eligible for publication - EX-1
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG46 - This meeting can no longer be published.
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return eligibility error
    MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG46 - This meeting can no longer be published.

else Eligible
    MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Change status to Published, record history (BR-37, BR-445)
    activate MeetingRepository
    MeetingRepository->Database: Save status and history
    activate Database
    Database-->MeetingRepository: Published
    deactivate Database
    MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Published
    deactivate MeetingRepository

    MeetingService->NotificationService: Notify participants and send invitations (BR-39, BR-40)
    activate NotificationService
    NotificationService-->MeetingService: Invitations queued
    deactivate NotificationService

    MeetingService-->MeetingController: Publication successful
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG45 - Meeting published successfully.
    MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG45 - Meeting published successfully.
end

deactivate MeetingService
deactivate MeetingController
deactivate MeetingPage
EOF
