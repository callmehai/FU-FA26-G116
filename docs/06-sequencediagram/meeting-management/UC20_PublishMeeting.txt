title UC-20 - Publish Meeting

actor "Meeting Organizer" as Organizer
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":User Repository" as UserRepository
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
participant ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
participant ":Notification Repository" as NotificationRepository
participant ":Meeting Invitation Queue" as InvitationQueue
database ":Database" as Database

Organizer->MeetingPage: Publish Approved meeting
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->MeetingController: Publish meeting
activate MeetingController
MeetingController->MeetingService: publishMeeting(meetingId, organizerId)
activate MeetingService

MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get meeting and current publication state
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting and Organizer assignment
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and publication state
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting and publication state
deactivate MeetingRepository

MeetingService->UserRepository: Verify current Organizer is active and has MeetingCreator
activate UserRepository
UserRepository->Database: Check Organizer account and capability
activate Database
Database-->UserRepository: Organizer eligibility
deactivate Database
UserRepository-->MeetingService: Organizer eligibility
deactivate UserRepository

MeetingService->ParticipantRepository: Get current Chair and Secretary assignments with active accounts (BR-124, BR-125)
activate ParticipantRepository
ParticipantRepository->Database: Retrieve current required-role holders and account status
activate Database
Database-->ParticipantRepository: Active Chair and Secretary assignments
deactivate Database
ParticipantRepository-->MeetingService: Required-role readiness
deactivate ParticipantRepository

MeetingService->MeetingService: Revalidate Organizer, Approved status,\nexactly one active current Chair and at least one active Secretary (BR-37, BR-124, BR-125)

alt Meeting is not Approved, Organizer is ineligible, or required active Chair/Secretary is missing - EX-1
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG46 - This meeting can no longer be published
    deactivate MeetingService
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return publication restriction
    deactivate MeetingController
    MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG46 - This meeting can no longer be published

else Organizer is eligible, meeting is Approved, and required active roles exist
    activate MeetingController
    activate MeetingService
    MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Revalidate publication readiness; record Published status\nand publication history together (BR-37, BR-38, BR-41, BR-124, BR-125)
    activate MeetingRepository
    MeetingRepository->Database: Save Published status and publication history as one complete action
    activate Database

        alt Publication cannot be completed - EX-2
            Database-->MeetingRepository: Status or history could not be completed; Meeting remains Approved and previous information unchanged
            deactivate Database
            MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Publication failed
            deactivate MeetingRepository
            MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG47 - Unable to publish the meeting. Please try again
            deactivate MeetingService
            MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return publication failure; no notification or invitation issued
            deactivate MeetingController
            MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG47 - Unable to publish the meeting. Please try again

        else Meeting published
            activate Database
            activate MeetingController
            activate MeetingRepository
            activate MeetingService
            Database-->MeetingRepository: Published status and publication history recorded together
            deactivate Database
            MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Published meeting
            deactivate MeetingRepository


            MeetingService->NotificationService: Notify current internal participants in the application (BR-39)
            activate NotificationService
            NotificationService->NotificationRepository: Create internal participant invitation notifications
            activate NotificationRepository
            NotificationRepository->Database: Record internal participant notifications
            activate Database
            Database-->NotificationRepository: Notifications recorded / delivery unavailable
            deactivate Database
            NotificationRepository-->NotificationService: Notification result
            deactivate NotificationRepository
            NotificationService-->MeetingService: Notifications issued / retry delivery; Meeting remains Published (BR-40)
            deactivate NotificationService

            MeetingService->InvitationQueue: Send invitation emails to current internal participants (BR-39)
            activate InvitationQueue
            InvitationQueue-->MeetingService: Email delivery arranged / retry delivery; Meeting remains Published (BR-40)
            deactivate InvitationQueue

            MeetingService->InvitationQueue: Send a separate email invitation to each current external Guest\nto verify the invited email address and access only this Meeting (BR-39, BR-152, BR-153)
            activate InvitationQueue
            InvitationQueue-->MeetingService: Guest invitation delivery arranged / retry delivery; Meeting remains Published (BR-40)
            deactivate InvitationQueue

            MeetingService-->MeetingController: Publication successful
            deactivate MeetingService
            MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG45 - Meeting published successfully
            deactivate MeetingController
            MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG45 - Meeting published successfully
    end
end
deactivate MeetingPage
