title UC-21 - End Meeting

actor "Chair" as Chair
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Chair->MeetingPage: End meeting
activate MeetingPage

MeetingPage->MeetingController: End meeting (meetingId)
activate MeetingController

MeetingController->MeetingService: endMeeting(meetingId, chair)
activate MeetingService

MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Find meeting and verify chair
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting and chair
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and chair
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting and chair
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt Meeting cannot be ended - EX-1
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG114 - This meeting cannot be ended.
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: Return end error
    MeetingPage-->Chair: MSG114 - This meeting cannot be ended.

else Eligible
    MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Complete agenda, delete draft votes, change status to Ended (BR-026, BR-086, BR-087, BR-022, BR-023)
    activate MeetingRepository
    MeetingRepository->Database: Save status, agenda, votes, history
    activate Database
    Database-->MeetingRepository: Ended
    deactivate Database
    MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Ended
    deactivate MeetingRepository

    MeetingService-->MeetingController: End successful
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG111 - Meeting ended successfully.
    MeetingPage-->Chair: MSG111 - Meeting ended successfully.
end

deactivate MeetingService
deactivate MeetingController
deactivate MeetingPage
