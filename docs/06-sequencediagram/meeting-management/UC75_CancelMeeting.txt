title UC-75 - Cancel Meeting

actor "Meeting Organizer" as Organizer
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Controller" as MeetingController
control ":Meeting Service" as MeetingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
participant ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
participant ":Meeting Invitation Queue" as InvitationQueue
database ":Database" as Database

Organizer->MeetingPage: Confirm Cancel meeting with a cancellation reason
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->MeetingController: Cancel meeting
activate MeetingController
MeetingController->MeetingService: cancelMeeting(meetingId, organizerId, reason)
activate MeetingService

MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Get Meeting, its Organizer and current status
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and status
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Meeting and status
deactivate MeetingRepository

MeetingService->MeetingService: Verify actor is the Meeting Organizer and Meeting is Draft,\nWaitingApproval, Rejected, Approved or Published (BR-297)
alt Meeting can no longer be cancelled - EX-2
    MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG263 - This meeting can no longer be cancelled.
    MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG263 - This meeting can no longer be cancelled.
    MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG263 - This meeting can no longer be cancelled.
else Cancellation permitted
    MeetingService->MeetingService: Validate cancellation reason is not blank (BR-298)
    alt Cancellation reason is missing - EX-1
        MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG262 - Enter a reason for cancelling the meeting.
        MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG262 - Enter a reason for cancelling the meeting.
        MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG262 - Enter a reason for cancelling the meeting.
    else Reason provided
        MeetingService->MeetingRepository: Recheck status, set Cancelled, record reason, actor and time\nin status history and close any pending approval request together (BR-38, BR-298, BR-299)
        activate MeetingRepository
        MeetingRepository->Database: Save status, history and approval request as one change
        activate Database
        Database-->MeetingRepository: Cancelled / Save failed; Meeting unchanged
        deactivate Database
        MeetingRepository-->MeetingService: Cancelled / Save failed; Meeting unchanged
        deactivate MeetingRepository
        alt Cancellation cannot be saved - EX-3
            MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG264 - Unable to cancel the meeting. Please try again.
            MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG264 - Unable to cancel the meeting. Please try again.
            MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG264 - Unable to cancel the meeting. Please try again.
        else Meeting cancelled
            opt Meeting was Published
                MeetingService->ParticipantRepository: Get current invited internal participants and Guests
                activate ParticipantRepository
                ParticipantRepository->Database: Get invited internal participants and Guests
                activate Database
                Database-->ParticipantRepository: Invitees
                deactivate Database
                ParticipantRepository-->MeetingService: Invitees
                deactivate ParticipantRepository
                MeetingService->NotificationService: Notify invited internal participants in the application with the reason (BR-300)
                activate NotificationService
                NotificationService-->MeetingService: Notifications created / delivery failed\n(failure does not undo the cancellation)
                deactivate NotificationService
                MeetingService->InvitationQueue: Queue cancellation emails to invited internal participants and Guests\nwith the reason (BR-300)
                activate InvitationQueue
                InvitationQueue-->MeetingService: Delivery arranged / retry delivery;\nMeeting remains Cancelled
                deactivate InvitationQueue
            end
            MeetingService-->MeetingController: MSG261 - Meeting cancelled successfully.
            MeetingController-->MeetingPage: MSG261 - Meeting cancelled successfully.
            MeetingPage-->Organizer: MSG261 - Meeting cancelled successfully.
        end
    end
end
deactivate MeetingService
deactivate MeetingController
deactivate MeetingPage
