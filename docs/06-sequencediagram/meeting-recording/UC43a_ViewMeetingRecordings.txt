title UC-43a - View Meeting Recordings

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Recordings Tab" as RecordingsTab
control ":Speech Clip Controller" as ClipController
control ":Recording Controller" as RecordingController
control ":Speech Clip Service" as ClipService
control ":Recording Service" as RecordingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Recording Repository" as RecordingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecordingsTab: Open the Recordings tab of a Meeting
activate RecordingsTab

RecordingsTab->ClipController: Get the Meeting speech timeline
activate ClipController
ClipController->ClipService: list(meetingId, actorId, since)
activate ClipService
ClipService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its format, status and current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ClipService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
ClipService->ClipService: Check the actor is an internal user who is the current Chair,\nSecretary or a Participant of the Meeting (BR-350, BR-370)

alt Actor is not on the current roster - EX-3
    ClipService-->ClipController: Meeting not found
    ClipController-->RecordingsTab: Meeting not found
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: Show the Meeting as not found
else Actor may listen
    ClipService->ClipRepository: Get the Meeting sentences in spoken order
    activate ClipRepository
    ClipRepository->Database: Retrieve sentences with speaker, text and audio
    activate Database
    Database-->ClipRepository: Sentences / Load failed
    deactivate Database
    ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentences / Load failed
    deactivate ClipRepository

    alt Sentences cannot be loaded - EX-2
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG299 - Unable to load transcript.
        ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG299 - Unable to load transcript.
        RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG299 - Unable to load transcript.
    else Sentences loaded
        ClipService->ClipService: Mark a sentence that repeats a neighbour's speech picked up\nby another microphone, without hiding it (BR-356)
        opt Actor is not the current Chair or Secretary - AF-2
            ClipService->ClipService: Keep each excluded sentence as an empty line:\nno words, no audio (BR-357)
        end
        ClipService-->ClipController: Speech timeline
        ClipController-->RecordingsTab: Speech timeline

        RecordingsTab->RecordingController: Get the Meeting room recordings
        activate RecordingController
        RecordingController->RecordingService: list(meetingId, actorId)
        activate RecordingService
        RecordingService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its format, status and current roster
        activate MeetingRepository
        MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
        activate Database
        Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
        deactivate Database
        MeetingRepository-->RecordingService: Meeting and roster
        deactivate MeetingRepository
        RecordingService->RecordingService: Check the actor may listen (BR-350, BR-370)
        RecordingService->RecordingRepository: Get the Meeting recordings with their takes and transcript status
        activate RecordingRepository
        RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve recordings
        activate Database
        Database-->RecordingRepository: Recordings / Load failed
        deactivate Database
        RecordingRepository-->RecordingService: Recordings / Load failed
        deactivate RecordingRepository

        alt Recordings cannot be loaded - EX-1
            RecordingService-->RecordingController: MSG297 - Unable to load meeting recordings.
            RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: MSG297 - Unable to load meeting recordings.
            RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG297 - Unable to load meeting recordings.
        else Recordings loaded
            RecordingService->RecordingService: Decide what the actor may do: record the room and upload\nonly for the current Secretary of an In-person or Hybrid Meeting,\ndownload only for the current Chair or Secretary (BR-340, BR-341, BR-342, BR-351)
            RecordingService-->RecordingController: Recordings with status, source, takes, retention date\nand transcript status
            RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: Recordings with status, source, takes, retention date\nand transcript status
            alt Meeting is Online - AF-1
                RecordingsTab-->Actor: Show the speech timeline only, without room recording or upload controls
            else Meeting is In-person or Hybrid
                RecordingsTab-->Actor: Show the speech timeline and the room recordings,\nwith a Recording badge while a room take is open
            end
        end
        deactivate RecordingService
        deactivate RecordingController
    end
end

deactivate ClipService
deactivate ClipController
deactivate RecordingsTab
