title UC-44b - Stop and Save Room Recording

actor "Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Room Recorder Card" as RecorderCard
control ":Recording Controller" as RecordingController
control ":Room Take Service" as RoomTakeService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Recording Repository" as RecordingRepository
participant ":Transcript Repository" as TranscriptRepository
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecorderCard: Turn the room microphone off, or save a take left open on another device
activate RecorderCard
RecorderCard->RecorderCard: Send the parts still kept on this device (UC-44a)
RecorderCard->RecordingController: Save the take
activate RecordingController
RecordingController->RoomTakeService: complete(recordingId, actorId)
activate RoomTakeService

RoomTakeService->MeetingRepository: Lock the Meeting and get its current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting for update with current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Lock the take
activate RecordingRepository
RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve take for update
activate Database
Database-->RecordingRepository: Take
deactivate Database
RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Take
deactivate RecordingRepository
RoomTakeService->RoomTakeService: Check the actor opened the take, or is the current Secretary\nsaving a take left open on another device - AF-3 (BR-341)

alt Actor may not save this take - EX-2
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
else Take is no longer open - EX-1
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG362 - This recording take has been closed and cannot accept additional audio.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG362 - This recording take has been closed and cannot accept additional audio.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG362 - This recording take has been closed and cannot accept additional audio.
else Take received no audio - AF-1
    RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Remove the empty take
    activate RecordingRepository
    RecordingRepository->Database: Delete take
    activate Database
    Database-->RecordingRepository: Take removed
    deactivate Database
    RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Take removed
    deactivate RecordingRepository
    RoomTakeService->RecordingStorage: Delete the empty take file
    activate RecordingStorage
    RecordingStorage-->RoomTakeService: File deleted
    deactivate RecordingStorage
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG365 - Microphone detected no speech; take was not saved.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG365 - Microphone detected no speech; take was not saved.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG365 - Microphone detected no speech; take was not saved.
else Take holds audio
    RoomTakeService->RecordingStorage: Close the take file at its final length
    activate RecordingStorage
    RecordingStorage-->RoomTakeService: Take file closed
    deactivate RecordingStorage
    RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Lock the Meeting room recording
    activate RecordingRepository
    RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve the newest kept room recording for update
    activate Database
    Database-->RecordingRepository: Room recording / None
    deactivate Database
    RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Room recording / None
    deactivate RecordingRepository

    alt First take of the Meeting, or the room recording is full - AF-4
        RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Make the take an Available room recording with one take
        activate RecordingRepository
        RecordingRepository->Database: Update take
        activate Database
        Database-->RecordingRepository: Recording saved / Save failed
        deactivate Database
        RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Recording saved / Save failed
        deactivate RecordingRepository
        RoomTakeService->TranscriptRepository: Queue a Pending transcript for the recording (BR-345)
        activate TranscriptRepository
        TranscriptRepository->Database: Insert Pending transcript
        activate Database
        Database-->TranscriptRepository: Transcript queued
        deactivate Database
        TranscriptRepository-->RoomTakeService: Transcript queued
        deactivate TranscriptRepository
    else Room recording exists
        RoomTakeService->RecordingStorage: Append the take audio to the room recording audio
        activate RecordingStorage
        RecordingStorage-->RoomTakeService: Joined audio / Storage failed
        deactivate RecordingStorage
        RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Add the take to the room recording with its place and length,\nupdate the duration and remove the separate take (BR-345)
        activate RecordingRepository
        RecordingRepository->Database: Update room recording, delete take
        activate Database
        Database-->RecordingRepository: Recording saved / Save failed
        deactivate Database
        RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Recording saved / Save failed
        deactivate RecordingRepository
        RoomTakeService->TranscriptRepository: Put the whole recording back as Pending to transcribe it again (BR-345)
        activate TranscriptRepository
        TranscriptRepository->Database: Update transcript to Pending
        activate Database
        Database-->TranscriptRepository: Transcript queued
        deactivate Database
        TranscriptRepository-->RoomTakeService: Transcript queued
        deactivate TranscriptRepository
    end
    RoomTakeService->AuditService: Record that the take was saved
    activate AuditService
    AuditService-->RoomTakeService: Entry saved
    deactivate AuditService

    alt Recording cannot be saved - EX-3
        RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
        RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
        RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
    else Take saved
        RoomTakeService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the recordings changed
        activate Publisher
        Publisher-->RoomTakeService: Change published
        deactivate Publisher
        RoomTakeService->RoomTakeService: ref UC-45c - the queued transcript is processed in the background
        RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG363 - Room recording take saved.
        RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG363 - Room recording take saved.
        RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG363 - Room recording take saved.
    end
end

deactivate RoomTakeService
deactivate RecordingController
deactivate RecorderCard
