title UC-43c - Download Meeting Recording

actor "Chair / Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Recordings Tab" as RecordingsTab
control ":Recording Controller" as RecordingController
control ":Recording Service" as RecordingService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Recording Repository" as RecordingRepository
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Playback Link Signer" as Signer
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecordingsTab: Download an available room recording
activate RecordingsTab
RecordingsTab->RecordingController: Get a download link for the recording
activate RecordingController
RecordingController->RecordingService: download(recordingId, actorId)
activate RecordingService

RecordingService->RecordingRepository: Get the recording
activate RecordingRepository
RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve recording
activate Database
Database-->RecordingRepository: Recording / Not found
deactivate Database
RecordingRepository-->RecordingService: Recording / Not found
deactivate RecordingRepository
RecordingService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its sensitivity and current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->RecordingService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
RecordingService->RecordingService: Check the actor is on the current roster and the recording is Available (BR-350),\nthen that the actor is the current Chair or Secretary and the Meeting is not Secret (BR-351)

alt Actor not on the roster, or recording not Available
    RecordingService-->RecordingController: Recording not found
    RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: Recording not found
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: Show the recording as no longer available
else Actor is not the current Chair or Secretary - EX-1
    RecordingService-->RecordingController: MSG355 - Only the Chair or Secretary may download meeting recordings and transcripts.
    RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: MSG355 - Only the Chair or Secretary may download meeting recordings and transcripts.
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG355 - Only the Chair or Secretary may download meeting recordings and transcripts.
else Meeting is Secret - EX-2
    RecordingService-->RecordingController: MSG356 - Recordings and transcripts of Secret meetings cannot be downloaded.
    RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: MSG356 - Recordings and transcripts of Secret meetings cannot be downloaded.
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG356 - Recordings and transcripts of Secret meetings cannot be downloaded.
else Download allowed
    RecordingService->AuditService: Record the download with the actor and time (BR-351)
    activate AuditService
    AuditService->Database: Insert audit entry
    activate Database
    Database-->AuditService: Entry saved
    deactivate Database
    AuditService-->RecordingService: Entry saved
    deactivate AuditService
    RecordingService->Signer: Sign a short-lived download link, separate from playback links
    activate Signer
    Signer-->RecordingService: Download link
    deactivate Signer
    RecordingService-->RecordingController: Download link
    RecordingController-->RecordingsTab: Download link
    RecordingsTab->RecordingStorage: Fetch the original file through the download link
    activate RecordingStorage
    RecordingStorage-->RecordingsTab: Original audio file
    deactivate RecordingStorage
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: The browser saves the original audio file
end

deactivate RecordingService
deactivate RecordingController
deactivate RecordingsTab
