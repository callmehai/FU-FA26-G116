title UC-80 - Manage My Recorded Speech

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "My Recorded Speech Panel" as MySpeechPanel
control ":Speech Clip Controller" as ClipController
control ":Speech Clip Service" as ClipService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->MySpeechPanel: Confirm Delete on one of the own saved sentences
activate MySpeechPanel
MySpeechPanel->ClipController: Delete the sentence
activate ClipController
ClipController->ClipService: delete(clipId, actorId)
activate ClipService

ClipService->ClipRepository: Get the sentence
activate ClipRepository
ClipRepository->Database: Retrieve sentence
activate Database
Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence / Not found
deactivate Database
ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentence / Not found
deactivate ClipRepository
ClipService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ClipService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
ClipService->ClipService: Check the actor is an internal user on the current roster (BR-350, BR-370)\nand is the speaker of the sentence or the current Secretary (BR-355)

alt Sentence no longer exists or actor is not on the roster
    ClipService-->ClipController: Sentence not found
    ClipController-->MySpeechPanel: Sentence not found
    MySpeechPanel-->Actor: Remove the sentence from the list
else Actor is neither the speaker nor the Secretary - EX-1
    ClipService-->ClipController: MSG323 - Only the speaker or the Secretary may delete this sentence.
    ClipController-->MySpeechPanel: MSG323 - Only the speaker or the Secretary may delete this sentence.
    MySpeechPanel-->Actor: MSG323 - Only the speaker or the Secretary may delete this sentence.
else Speaker withdraws the own sentence
    ClipService->AuditService: Record the deletion, made by the speaker (BR-355)
    activate AuditService
    AuditService-->ClipService: Entry saved
    deactivate AuditService
    ClipService->ClipRepository: Delete the sentence with its text
    activate ClipRepository
    ClipRepository->Database: Delete sentence
    activate Database
    Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence deleted / Save failed
    deactivate Database
    ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentence deleted / Save failed
    deactivate ClipRepository

    alt Sentence cannot be deleted - EX-2
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
        ClipController-->MySpeechPanel: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
        MySpeechPanel-->Actor: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
    else Sentence deleted
        ClipService->RecordingStorage: Delete the sentence audio (BR-355)
        activate RecordingStorage
        RecordingStorage-->ClipService: Audio deleted
        deactivate RecordingStorage
        ClipService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the speech timeline changed
        activate Publisher
        Publisher-->ClipService: Change published
        deactivate Publisher
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG322 - Spoken sentence deleted.
        ClipController-->MySpeechPanel: MSG322 - Spoken sentence deleted.
        MySpeechPanel-->Actor: MSG322 - Spoken sentence deleted.
    end
end

deactivate ClipService
deactivate ClipController
deactivate MySpeechPanel
