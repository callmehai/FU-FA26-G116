title SYS - Build Combined Recording of a Hybrid Meeting (System, DRAFT awaiting approval - no UC number)

actor "System" as System
control ":Combined Recording Worker" as Worker
control ":Combined Recording Service" as CombinedService
participant ":Recording Repository" as RecordingRepository
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

System->Worker: Sweep every 30 seconds for a Hybrid Meeting waiting for its combined recording
activate Worker
Worker->CombinedService: buildNext()
activate CombinedService
CombinedService->RecordingRepository: Get Ended Hybrid Meetings settled for one minute and changed within 8 days
activate RecordingRepository
RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve candidate Ended Hybrid Meetings
activate Database
Database-->RecordingRepository: Candidate Meetings / None
deactivate Database
RecordingRepository-->CombinedService: Candidate Meetings / None
deactivate RecordingRepository

alt No Meeting needs its combined recording
    CombinedService-->Worker: Nothing to do
else A Meeting needs its combined recording
    CombinedService->RecordingRepository: Get the Meeting recordings
    activate RecordingRepository
    RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve recordings
    activate Database
    Database-->RecordingRepository: Recordings
    deactivate Database
    RecordingRepository-->CombinedService: Recordings
    deactivate RecordingRepository
    CombinedService->ClipRepository: Get the Meeting online sentences with audio that are not excluded
    activate ClipRepository
    ClipRepository->Database: Retrieve kept sentences in spoken order
    activate Database
    Database-->ClipRepository: Sentences
    deactivate Database
    ClipRepository-->CombinedService: Sentences
    deactivate ClipRepository

    alt Take still streaming, source too new, erased, or one-sided - nothing to build yet
        CombinedService-->Worker: Nothing to do
    else Sources settled and both room and online sound exist
        CombinedService->CombinedService: Lay room takes and online sentences on the real clock,\ncut silence over 2 seconds to 2 seconds (BR-456)
        alt Combined track is longer than 12 hours
            CombinedService-->Worker: Not built
        else Combined track fits
            CombinedService->RecordingStorage: Mix room and online sound into one WAV file
            activate RecordingStorage
            RecordingStorage-->CombinedService: Combined file
            deactivate RecordingStorage
            CombinedService->RecordingRepository: Add or replace the Available Combined recording\nwith the same purpose and retention as the room recording (BR-347, BR-456)
            activate RecordingRepository
            RecordingRepository->Database: Insert or update Combined recording
            activate Database
            Database-->RecordingRepository: Recording saved
            deactivate Database
            RecordingRepository-->CombinedService: Recording saved
            deactivate RecordingRepository
            CombinedService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the recordings changed
            activate Publisher
            Publisher-->CombinedService: Change published
            deactivate Publisher
            CombinedService-->Worker: Combined recording built or rebuilt
        end
    end
end

deactivate CombinedService
deactivate Worker
