title UC-44a - Start Room Recording

actor "Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Room Recorder Card" as RecorderCard
control ":Recording Controller" as RecordingController
control ":Room Take Service" as RoomTakeService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Recording Repository" as RecordingRepository
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecorderCard: Turn the room microphone on, confirming before the first take\nthat attendees were informed and nobody objected
activate RecorderCard
RecorderCard->RecordingController: Open a room take with the confirmation and purpose
activate RecordingController
RecordingController->RoomTakeService: begin(meetingId, actorId, start)
activate RoomTakeService

RoomTakeService->MeetingRepository: Lock the Meeting and get its format, status and current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting for update with current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Get the Meeting recordings and its room recording
activate RecordingRepository
RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve recordings and the newest kept room recording
activate Database
Database-->RecordingRepository: Recordings, open take, room recording
deactivate Database
RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Recordings, open take, room recording
deactivate RecordingRepository
RoomTakeService->RoomTakeService: Check current Secretary (BR-341), In-person or Hybrid (BR-340),\nIn Progress (BR-342), no open take (BR-344) and, before the first take only,\nthe attendee notice confirmation (BR-343)

alt Actor is not the current Secretary - EX-2
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG295 - Only the meeting Secretary may manage recordings.
else Meeting is Online - EX-4
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG358 - Online meetings record per-mic speech; room recordings are not available.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG358 - Online meetings record per-mic speech; room recordings are not available.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG358 - Online meetings record per-mic speech; room recordings are not available.
else Meeting is not In Progress - EX-5
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG359 - Room microphone can only be turned on while the meeting is in progress.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG359 - Room microphone can only be turned on while the meeting is in progress.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG359 - Room microphone can only be turned on while the meeting is in progress.
else A take is already open on another device - EX-6
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG360 - Room microphone is currently active on another device.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG360 - Room microphone is currently active on another device.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG360 - Room microphone is currently active on another device.
else First take without the notice confirmation, or purpose too long - EX-1
    RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG291 - Invalid recording information.
    RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG291 - Invalid recording information.
    RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG291 - Invalid recording information.
else Take can be opened (a later take needs no confirmation - AF-2)
    RoomTakeService->RecordingStorage: Create an empty audio file for the take
    activate RecordingStorage
    RecordingStorage-->RoomTakeService: New file / Storage failed
    deactivate RecordingStorage
    RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Add an open take with the confirmation time and purpose\nof the room recording, kept for 180 days (BR-343, BR-344, BR-347)
    activate RecordingRepository
    RecordingRepository->Database: Insert open take
    activate Database
    Database-->RecordingRepository: Take saved / Save failed
    deactivate Database
    RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Take saved / Save failed
    deactivate RecordingRepository
    RoomTakeService->AuditService: Record that the take started, and whether it is the first
    activate AuditService
    AuditService-->RoomTakeService: Entry saved
    deactivate AuditService

    alt Take cannot be opened - EX-3
        RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
        RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
        RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG298 - Unable to save recording changes. Please try again.
    else Take opened
        RoomTakeService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the room is being recorded
        activate Publisher
        Publisher-->RoomTakeService: Change published
        deactivate Publisher
        RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: Open take with its part size and size limit
        RecordingController-->RecorderCard: Open take with its part size and size limit
        RecorderCard-->Actor: Show the room microphone on and the Recording badge

        loop While the room microphone is on
            RecorderCard->RecorderCard: Keep the recorded audio on this device until each part is accepted
            RecorderCard->RecordingController: Send the next audio part
            RecordingController->RoomTakeService: append(recordingId, actorId, part, content, length, final)
            RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Lock the open take
            activate RecordingRepository
            RecordingRepository->Database: Retrieve take for update
            activate Database
            Database-->RecordingRepository: Open take
            deactivate Database
            RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Open take
            deactivate RecordingRepository
            RoomTakeService->RoomTakeService: Check the part follows the previous one and has the expected size
            alt Audio part is invalid - EX-7
                RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: MSG361 - The recorded audio is not valid.
                RecordingController-->RecorderCard: MSG361 - The recorded audio is not valid.
                RecorderCard-->Actor: MSG361 - The recorded audio is not valid.
            else Part accepted
                RoomTakeService->RecordingStorage: Write the part at its place in the take file
                activate RecordingStorage
                RecordingStorage-->RoomTakeService: Part written
                deactivate RecordingStorage
                RoomTakeService->RecordingRepository: Save the take size and duration
                activate RecordingRepository
                RecordingRepository->Database: Update take
                activate Database
                Database-->RecordingRepository: Take updated
                deactivate Database
                RecordingRepository-->RoomTakeService: Take updated
                deactivate RecordingRepository
                RoomTakeService-->RecordingController: Part accepted
                RecordingController-->RecorderCard: Part accepted
                opt Take nears its size limit - AF-3
                    RecorderCard->RecorderCard: ref UC-44b - save this take, then open the next take\nwithout stopping the recording (BR-345)
                end
            end
        end
    end
end

deactivate RoomTakeService
deactivate RecordingController
deactivate RecorderCard
