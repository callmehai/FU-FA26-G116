title UC-52a - View Meeting Votes

actor "User" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Vote Controller" as VoteController
control ":Vote Service" as VoteService
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
participant ":Meeting Access Policy" as MeetingAccessPolicy
participant ":Vote Repository" as VoteRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->MeetingPage: Open the Voting area of a Meeting
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->VoteController: View Meeting Votes
activate VoteController
VoteController->VoteService: listMeetingVotes(meetingId, actorId)
activate VoteService

VoteService->MeetingAccessPolicy: Check actor can currently view the Meeting's Votes (BR-29, BR-181, BR-274)
activate MeetingAccessPolicy
MeetingAccessPolicy->Database: Check current Meeting relationship or valid Guest invitation
activate Database
Database-->MeetingAccessPolicy: Access / No access
deactivate Database
MeetingAccessPolicy-->VoteService: Access / No access
deactivate MeetingAccessPolicy
alt Meeting unavailable or no current permission to view its Votes - EX-1
    VoteService-->VoteController: MSG205 - Meeting votes are not accessible.
    VoteController-->MeetingPage: MSG205 - Meeting votes are not accessible.
    MeetingPage-->Actor: MSG205 - Meeting votes are not accessible.
else Meeting Votes are accessible
    VoteService->ParticipantRepository: Check current Chair or Secretary assignment (BR-253)
    activate ParticipantRepository
    ParticipantRepository->Database: Check current Chair or Secretary assignment (BR-253)
    activate Database
    Database-->ParticipantRepository: Current Chair or Secretary / Not eligible
    deactivate Database
    ParticipantRepository-->VoteService: Current Chair or Secretary / Not eligible
    deactivate ParticipantRepository
    VoteService->VoteRepository: Get Votes of the Meeting; exclude Draft Votes unless the actor is the\ncurrent Chair or Secretary (BR-253, BR-285)
    activate VoteRepository
    VoteRepository->Database: Retrieve Votes of the Meeting with visibility filter
    activate Database
    Database-->VoteRepository: Visible Votes with content, ballot type, status, turnout of Open Votes\nand conclusion of Closed Votes / Load failed (BR-260)
    deactivate Database
    VoteRepository-->VoteService: Visible Votes with content, ballot type, status, turnout of Open Votes\nand conclusion of Closed Votes / Load failed (BR-260)
    deactivate VoteRepository
    alt Votes cannot be loaded - EX-2
        VoteService-->VoteController: MSG206 - Unable to load the meeting votes. Please try again.
        VoteController-->MeetingPage: MSG206 - Unable to load the meeting votes. Please try again.
        MeetingPage-->Actor: MSG206 - Unable to load the meeting votes. Please try again.
    else Votes are loaded
        alt No Vote is visible to the actor - AF-1
            VoteService-->VoteController: MSG207 - No votes to display.
            VoteController-->MeetingPage: MSG207 - No votes to display.
            MeetingPage-->Actor: MSG207 - No votes to display.
        else At least one Vote is visible
            VoteService-->VoteController: Visible Votes (counts include only visible Votes)
            VoteController-->MeetingPage: Visible Votes
            MeetingPage-->Actor: Show visible Votes with content, ballot type and status
        end
    end
end

deactivate VoteService
deactivate VoteController
deactivate MeetingPage
