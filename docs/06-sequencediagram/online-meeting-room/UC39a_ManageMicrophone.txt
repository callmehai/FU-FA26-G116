title UC-39a - Manage Microphone

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Turn microphone on or off (button or Ctrl+D)
activate Page
alt The browser blocks the microphone - EX-1
    Page-->Actor: MSG334 - The browser is blocking the microphone. Click the lock icon beside the address bar,\nchoose Allow and reload the page. You can still join the room to listen.
else No microphone is available - EX-2
    Page-->Actor: MSG335 - No microphone was found on this computer.
else Another application holds the microphone - EX-3
    Page-->Actor: MSG336 - The microphone is being used by another application (Zoom, Teams...).\nClose that application and try again.
else Microphone can be switched
    Page->RoomProvider: Publish or mute the actor's own microphone
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->Page: Everyone in the room sees the microphone state (UC-40)
    deactivate RoomProvider
    Page->Page: Remember the on/off state for the next visit on this device (BR-331)
    Page-->Actor: Button and video tile show the new state
end
deactivate Page
