title UC-38 - Leave Meeting Room

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Confirm Leave
activate Page
alt Call is in the floating window - AF-2
    Page->Page: Leave at once without asking and close the floating window (BR-332)
else Call is in the main window - AF-1
    Page-->Actor: Ask for confirmation: Stay or Leave
end
Page->RoomProvider: Disconnect the actor from the meeting room
activate RoomProvider
RoomProvider-->Page: Disconnected
deactivate RoomProvider
Page->Page: Stop camera and microphone; the Meeting stays In Progress for everyone else (BR-324)
Page-->Actor: Meeting detail page
opt The connection ends without the actor choosing Leave
    alt The Meeting ended - EX-1
        Page-->Actor: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
    else Same account joined from another tab or device - EX-2 (BR-328)
        Page-->Actor: MSG329 - You joined this room from another tab or device, so this tab left the room.
    else Actor is no longer on the participant list - EX-3
        Page-->Actor: MSG330 - You are no longer on the participant list of this meeting room.
    else Connection is lost - EX-4
        Page-->Actor: MSG333 - Connection lost, reconnecting...
        Page-->Actor: MSG332 - Connection to the meeting room lost.
    else This tab left the room without Leave - EX-5
        Page-->Actor: MSG328 - You have left the meeting room.
    end
end
deactivate Page
