title UC-38 - Leave Meeting Room

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Room Page" as RoomPage
control ":Meeting Room Controller" as RoomController
control ":Meeting Room Service" as RoomService
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RoomPage: Leave meeting room
activate RoomPage

RoomPage->RoomController: Leave room
activate RoomController

RoomController->RoomService: leaveMeetingRoom(meetingId, actorId)
activate RoomService

alt Meeting has ended while in the room - EX-1
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
    RoomController-->RoomPage: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
    RoomPage-->Actor: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.

else Same account joined elsewhere - EX-2
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG329 - You joined this room from another tab or device, so this tab left the room.
    RoomController-->RoomPage: MSG329 - You joined this room from another tab or device, so this tab left the room.
    RoomPage-->Actor: MSG329 - You joined this room from another tab or device, so this tab left the room.

else Actor is no longer on the participant list - EX-3
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG330 - You are no longer on the participant list of this meeting room.
    RoomController-->RoomPage: MSG330 - You are no longer on the participant list of this meeting room.
    RoomPage-->Actor: MSG330 - You are no longer on the participant list of this meeting room.

else Eligible
    RoomService->RoomProvider: Disconnect actor
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->RoomService: Disconnected
    deactivate RoomProvider

    RoomService-->RoomController: Left room
    RoomController-->RoomPage: MSG328 - You have left the meeting room.
    RoomPage-->Actor: MSG328 - You have left the meeting room.
end

deactivate RoomService
deactivate RoomController
deactivate RoomPage
