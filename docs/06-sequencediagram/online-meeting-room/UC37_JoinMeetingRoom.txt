title UC-37 - Join Meeting Room

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Lobby Page" as Page
control ":Meeting Room Controller" as RoomController
control ":Meeting Room Service" as RoomService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->Page: Choose Join room
activate Page
Page->RoomController: Join the Meeting room
activate RoomController
RoomController->RoomService: joinMeetingRoom(meetingId, actorId)
activate RoomService
RoomService->MeetingRepository: Read Meeting status, mode and the actor's current participant assignment
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and roster information
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Current information
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->RoomService: Current information
deactivate MeetingRepository
RoomService->RoomService: Verify the actor may still view the Meeting and holds a current room-entry\nrelationship; for a Guest verify the invitation (BR-97, BR-101, BR-152 – BR-157, BR-181, BR-323)
alt Actor is no longer eligible - EX-4
    RoomService-->RoomController: Meeting not found for the actor
    RoomController-->Page: Meeting not found for the actor
    Page-->Actor: Meeting page shown as for a Meeting that does not exist
else Meeting has ended - EX-1
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
    Page-->Actor: MSG331 - The meeting has ended, the meeting room is closed.
else Meeting is cancelled - EX-6
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG350 - The meeting was cancelled, so the meeting room does not open.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG350 - The meeting was cancelled, so the meeting room does not open.
    Page-->Actor: MSG350 - The meeting was cancelled, so the meeting room does not open.
else Meeting is held in person - EX-2
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG280 - This meeting is held in person, so it has no online meeting room.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG280 - This meeting is held in person, so it has no online meeting room.
    Page-->Actor: MSG280 - This meeting is held in person, so it has no online meeting room.
else Meeting has not started yet - AF-3
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.
    Page-->Actor: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.\nThe lobby opens by itself when the Meeting starts
else Room service is not configured - EX-3
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG281 - The online meeting room is not ready. Please contact the administrator.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG281 - The online meeting room is not ready. Please contact the administrator.
    Page-->Actor: MSG281 - The online meeting room is not ready. Please contact the administrator.
else Meeting is In Progress and the actor is eligible
    RoomService->RoomProvider: Request a room connection for the actor with the actor's current Meeting Role (BR-323, BR-329)
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->RoomService: Room connection / refused
    deactivate RoomProvider
    alt Join cannot be completed - EX-5
        RoomService-->RoomController: MSG118 - Unable to join the meeting room. Please try again.
        RoomController-->Page: MSG118 - Unable to join the meeting room. Please try again.
        Page-->Actor: MSG118 - Unable to join the meeting room. Please try again.
    else Room connection issued
        RoomService-->RoomController: Room connection
        RoomController-->Page: Room connection
        Page->RoomProvider: Connect to the meeting room (fewer than 50 people, BR-336; EX-7 when full)
        activate RoomProvider
        RoomProvider-->Page: People already in the room
        deactivate RoomProvider
        Page-->Actor: Online Meeting Room - Video Grid with the people in the room (UC-40)
        opt Browser blocks audio playback - AF-4
            Page-->Actor: MSG338 - The browser is blocking audio playback.
        end
    end
end
