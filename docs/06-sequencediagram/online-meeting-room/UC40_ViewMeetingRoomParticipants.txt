title UC-40 - View Meeting Room Participants

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Open the Participants panel
activate Page
Page->RoomProvider: Read the people connected now with their speaking and microphone state
activate RoomProvider
RoomProvider-->Page: People connected now
deactivate RoomProvider
Page->Page: Order by Meeting Role (Chair, Secretary, Member, Guest) then by name;\nmark the actor as You; presence is not attendance (BR-329, BR-330)
Page-->Actor: Participants panel with the number of people connected
opt People join, leave, speak, mute or change role - AF-1, AF-2, AF-3
    RoomProvider-->Page: Change in the room
    Page-->Actor: List and count update without a reload
end
opt Connection is lost - EX-1
    Page-->Actor: MSG333 - Connection lost, reconnecting...
end
deactivate Page
