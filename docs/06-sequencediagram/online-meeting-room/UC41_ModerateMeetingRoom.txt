title UC-41 - Moderate Meeting Room

actor "Chair" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
control ":Meeting Room Controller" as RoomController
control ":Meeting Room Service" as RoomService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->Page: Choose Mute on a person whose microphone is on
activate Page
Page->RoomController: Mute the selected person's microphone
activate RoomController
RoomController->RoomService: muteParticipant(meetingId, chairId, targetUserId)
activate RoomService
RoomService->MeetingRepository: Read Meeting status and the current Chair
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and Chair information
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Current information
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->RoomService: Current information
deactivate MeetingRepository
RoomService->RoomService: Verify the actor is the current Chair and the Meeting is In Progress (BR-325)
alt The actor is no longer the Chair - EX-1
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG123 - Only the Chair can mute another person's microphone.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG123 - Only the Chair can mute another person's microphone.
    Page-->Actor: MSG123 - Only the Chair can mute another person's microphone.
else The Meeting is no longer In Progress - EX-5
    RoomService-->RoomController: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.
    RoomController-->Page: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.
    Page-->Actor: MSG117 - The meeting room opens only while the meeting is in progress.
else The Chair may mute
    RoomService->RoomProvider: Mute the target's microphone for everyone in the room (BR-325)
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->RoomService: Muted / target not in the room or no microphone on / no response
    deactivate RoomProvider
    alt The target is no longer in the room or has no microphone on - EX-2
        RoomService-->RoomController: MSG282 - This person is no longer in the room or has no microphone on.
        RoomController-->Page: MSG282 - This person is no longer in the room or has no microphone on.
        Page-->Actor: MSG282 - This person is no longer in the room or has no microphone on.
    else The room service does not respond - EX-3
        RoomService-->RoomController: MSG124 - The meeting room server did not respond. Please try again.
        RoomController-->Page: MSG124 - The meeting room server did not respond. Please try again.
        Page-->Actor: MSG124 - The meeting room server did not respond. Please try again.
    else Muting cannot be saved - EX-4
        RoomService-->RoomController: MSG284 - Unable to mute the microphone. Please try again.
        RoomController-->Page: MSG284 - Unable to mute the microphone. Please try again.
        Page-->Actor: MSG284 - Unable to mute the microphone. Please try again.
    else Microphone muted
        RoomService-->RoomController: Microphone muted
        RoomController-->Page: Microphone muted
        Page-->Actor: MSG342 - Muted the microphone of {name}.
        RoomProvider-->Page: The target's microphone is off
        Page-->Actor: MSG339 - The Chair has muted your microphone. You can turn it back on when you need to speak.\n(shown to the target)
    end
end
deactivate RoomService
deactivate RoomController
deactivate Page
