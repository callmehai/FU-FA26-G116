title UC-39b - Manage Camera

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Turn camera on or off (button or Ctrl+E)
activate Page
alt The browser blocks the camera - EX-1
    Page-->Actor: MSG334 - The browser is blocking the camera. Click the lock icon beside the address bar,\nchoose Allow and reload the page. You can still join the room to listen.
else No camera is available - EX-2
    Page-->Actor: MSG335 - No camera was found on this computer.
else Another application holds the camera - EX-3
    Page-->Actor: MSG336 - The camera is being used by another application (Zoom, Teams...).\nClose that application and try again.
else Camera can be switched
    Page->RoomProvider: Publish or stop the actor's own camera
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->Page: Everyone in the room sees the video or the initials (UC-40)
    deactivate RoomProvider
    Page->Page: Remember the on/off state for the next visit on this device (BR-331)
    Page-->Actor: Button shows the new state
end
deactivate Page
