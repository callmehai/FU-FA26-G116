title UC-39a - Record Speech While Microphone On (AF-2)

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as RoomPage
control ":Speech Clip Controller" as ClipController
control ":Speech Clip Service" as ClipService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Speech Recognition Service" as SpeechEngine
participant ":Recording Storage" as RecordingStorage
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RoomPage: Speak a sentence while the own microphone is on
activate RoomPage
RoomPage->RoomPage: Cut the sentence at the pause and keep it on this device\nuntil the system accepts it (BR-352, BR-354)
RoomPage->ClipController: Send the spoken sentence
activate ClipController
ClipController->ClipService: canCapture(meetingId, actorId)
activate ClipService
ClipService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its format, status and current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ClipService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository
ClipService->ClipService: Check the actor is an internal user on the current roster (BR-350, BR-370)\nand the Meeting records per-mic speech: Online or Hybrid, In Progress\nor ended at most 7 days ago (BR-340, BR-354)

alt Meeting cannot be recorded now - EX-5
    ClipService-->ClipController: MSG320 - In-person meetings do not record per-mic speech, or the meeting is no longer running.
    ClipController-->RoomPage: MSG320 - In-person meetings do not record per-mic speech, or the meeting is no longer running.
    RoomPage->RoomPage: Drop the sentence from this device
    RoomPage-->Actor: MSG320 - In-person meetings do not record per-mic speech, or the meeting is no longer running.
else Meeting records per-mic speech
    ClipService-->ClipController: Sentence may be sent
    ClipController->ClipController: Check the sentence audio type, start time, duration and size
    alt Sentence audio is longer than one sentence allows - AF-2
        ClipController-->RoomPage: MSG321 - A spoken sentence cannot exceed 60 seconds.
        RoomPage->RoomPage: Drop the sentence from this device
        RoomPage-->Actor: MSG321 - A spoken sentence cannot exceed 60 seconds.
    else Sentence audio is invalid - AF-2
        ClipController-->RoomPage: MSG319 - Spoken sentence audio is invalid.
        RoomPage->RoomPage: Drop the sentence from this device
        RoomPage-->Actor: MSG319 - Spoken sentence audio is invalid.
    else Sentence audio is valid
        ClipController->ClipService: add(meetingId, actorId, upload)
        ClipService->SpeechEngine: Turn the sentence into text inside the system (BR-369)
        activate SpeechEngine
        SpeechEngine-->ClipService: Text with word timings / Service unavailable
        deactivate SpeechEngine

        alt Speech Recognition Service unavailable - EX-4
            ClipService-->ClipController: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
            ClipController-->RoomPage: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
            RoomPage->RoomPage: Keep the sentence on this device and send it again later (BR-354)
            RoomPage-->Actor: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
        else Only silence or noise - AF-2
            ClipService-->ClipController: Nothing to keep
            ClipController-->RoomPage: Nothing to keep
            RoomPage->RoomPage: Drop the sentence from this device
        else Words recognised
            ClipService->RecordingStorage: Store the sentence audio
            activate RecordingStorage
            RecordingStorage-->ClipService: Stored audio
            deactivate RecordingStorage
            ClipService->ClipRepository: Recheck the Meeting still accepts sentences and save the sentence\nwith the actor as speaker, its start time, text and audio,\nkept for 180 days (BR-347, BR-352, BR-354)
            activate ClipRepository
            ClipRepository->Database: Insert sentence
            activate Database
            Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence saved
            deactivate Database
            ClipRepository-->ClipService: Saved sentence
            deactivate ClipRepository
            ClipService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the speech timeline changed
            activate Publisher
            Publisher-->ClipService: Change published
            deactivate Publisher
            ClipService-->ClipController: MSG318 - Spoken sentence recorded.
            ClipController-->RoomPage: MSG318 - Spoken sentence recorded.
            RoomPage->RoomPage: Remove the sentence from this device
            RoomPage-->Actor: The sentence appears in the Meeting speech timeline (UC-43a) and in My Recorded Speech (UC-80)
        end
    end
end

deactivate ClipService
deactivate ClipController
deactivate RoomPage
