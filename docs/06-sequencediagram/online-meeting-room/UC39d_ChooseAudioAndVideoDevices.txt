title UC-39d - Choose Audio and Video Devices

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Lobby / Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Choose a microphone, speaker or camera
activate Page
alt The browser lists no device of that kind - AF-2
    Page-->Actor: Picker disabled and says why; the actor can still join the room to listen
else The chosen device is not found - EX-1
    Page-->Actor: MSG335 - No {device} was found on this computer.
else Another application holds the device - EX-2
    Page-->Actor: MSG336 - The {device} is being used by another application (Zoom, Teams...).\nClose that application and try again.
else The device cannot be opened - EX-3
    Page-->Actor: MSG337 - Unable to open the {device}. You can still join the room to listen.
else Device can be switched
    Page->RoomProvider: Switch the live microphone, speaker or camera and keep the on/off state
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->Page: Device in use
    deactivate RoomProvider
    Page->Page: Remember the choice for the next visit on this device; no Meeting setting changes (BR-331)
    Page-->Actor: Picker shows the new device
end
deactivate Page
