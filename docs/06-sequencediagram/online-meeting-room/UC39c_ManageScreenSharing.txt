title UC-39c - Manage Screen Sharing

actor "Participant" as Actor
boundary "Online Meeting Room Page" as Page
participant ":Room Service" as RoomProvider

Actor->Page: Choose Share screen and pick a screen, window or tab
activate Page
alt The sharing cannot start - EX-1
    Page-->Actor: MSG341 - Unable to share your screen. Please try again.
else Sharing starts
    Page->RoomProvider: Publish the shared screen; stop any earlier presentation (BR-326)
    activate RoomProvider
    RoomProvider-->Page: Presentation shown on the main stage for everyone
    deactivate RoomProvider
    Page-->Actor: You are presenting to everyone
end
opt Someone else starts presenting - AF-1
    RoomProvider-->Page: Another person is presenting
    Page-->Actor: MSG340 - {name} started presenting, so your screen share was stopped.
end
Actor->Page: Choose Stop sharing
Page->RoomProvider: Stop the presentation
RoomProvider-->Page: Everyone returns to the Video Grid
deactivate Page
