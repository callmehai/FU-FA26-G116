title UC-72c - Move Folder

actor "Internal User" as Actor
boundary "Personal Library Screen" as LibraryScreen
control ":Folder Controller" as FolderController
control ":Folder Service" as FolderService
participant ":Folder Repository" as FolderRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->LibraryScreen: Confirm the destination
activate LibraryScreen

LibraryScreen->FolderController: Move the folder to the destination
activate FolderController

FolderController->FolderService: moveFolder(ownerId, folderId, destinationId)
activate FolderService

FolderService->FolderRepository: Get the folder, its subfolders and the destination
activate FolderRepository
FolderRepository->Database: Retrieve the folder, its descendants and the destination
activate Database
Database-->FolderRepository: Folders / Not found
deactivate Database
FolderRepository-->FolderService: Folders / Not found
deactivate FolderRepository

alt Folder or destination is missing or not owned by the user - EX-1
    FolderService-->FolderController: MSG179 - The folder or destination is not available.
    FolderController-->LibraryScreen: MSG179 - The folder or destination is not available.
    LibraryScreen-->Actor: MSG179 - The folder or destination is not available.
else Destination is the folder itself or one of its subfolders - EX-2
    FolderService-->FolderController: MSG193 - A folder cannot be moved into itself or its subfolders.
    FolderController-->LibraryScreen: MSG193 - A folder cannot be moved into itself or its subfolders.
    LibraryScreen-->Actor: MSG193 - A folder cannot be moved into itself or its subfolders.
else Moved folders would be deeper than level five - EX-3
    FolderService-->FolderController: MSG189 - Folders cannot be nested more than five levels deep.
    FolderController-->LibraryScreen: MSG189 - Folders cannot be nested more than five levels deep.
    LibraryScreen-->Actor: MSG189 - Folders cannot be nested more than five levels deep.
else Move is valid
    FolderService->FolderRepository: Move the folder to the destination
    activate FolderRepository
    FolderRepository->Database: Update the parent of the folder
    activate Database
    Database-->FolderRepository: Saved
    deactivate Database
    FolderRepository-->FolderService: Moved folder
    deactivate FolderRepository

    FolderService-->FolderController: Moved folder
    FolderController-->LibraryScreen: Moved folder
    LibraryScreen-->Actor: MSG194 - Folder moved.
end

deactivate FolderService
deactivate FolderController
deactivate LibraryScreen
