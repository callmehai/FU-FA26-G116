title UC-39 - Assign / Change Meeting Role

actor "Secretary" as Secretary
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Participant Controller" as ParticipantController
control ":Meeting Participant Service" as ParticipantService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
participant ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
database ":Database" as Database

Secretary->MeetingPage: Submit selected Meeting Role
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->ParticipantController: Assign / Change Meeting Role
activate ParticipantController
ParticipantController->ParticipantService: changeMeetingRole(meetingId, participantId, secretaryId, meetingRole)
activate ParticipantService

ParticipantService->MeetingRepository: Get meeting and current status
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and status
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ParticipantService: Meeting and status
deactivate MeetingRepository

ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Get Secretary assignment, target participant,\nand current Chair and Secretary counts (BR-78, BR-84, BR-89)
activate ParticipantRepository
ParticipantRepository->Database: Retrieve current participant assignments
activate Database
Database-->ParticipantRepository: Assignment, participant, and role counts
deactivate Database
ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Assignment, participant, and role counts
deactivate ParticipantRepository

ParticipantService->ParticipantService: Validate assigned Secretary, editable status (excluding WaitingApproval), target participant,\nMeeting Role, and required-role protection (BR-78, BR-80, BR-89)

alt Meeting is WaitingApproval, Ended, MinutesIssued, or Archived - EX-3
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Participant list is read-only
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Modification rejected
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG98 - The meeting participant list cannot be modified in its current status.
else Submitted Meeting Role is invalid - EX-1
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Validation failed
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Invalid Meeting Role
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG101 - A valid meeting role is required.
else Selected Meeting Role is unchanged - AF-1
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: No change required
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Request completed
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG100 - Meeting role updated successfully.
else Change would remove the last required-role holder - EX-2
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Required Meeting Role would be removed
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Role change rejected
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG109 - The meeting must retain its required Chair and Secretary.\nAssign a replacement first.
else Meeting Role can be changed
    ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Update only this meeting's Meeting Role;\nkeep system Role and Invitation Response unchanged (BR-80, BR-83)
    activate ParticipantRepository
    ParticipantRepository->Database: Update Meeting Participant role
    activate Database
    Database-->ParticipantRepository: Role updated / Save failed
    deactivate Database
    ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Updated participant / Save failed
    deactivate ParticipantRepository

    alt Meeting Role cannot be updated - EX-4
        ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Save failed
        deactivate ParticipantService
        ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Update failed
        deactivate ParticipantController
        MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG102 - Unable to update the meeting role. Please try again.
    else Meeting Role is updated
        opt Meeting is Published or InProgress
            ParticipantService->NotificationService: Queue role-change notification and email;\nrecord delivery failure for retry without rollback (BR-82)
            activate NotificationService
            NotificationService-->ParticipantService: Queued / Retry recorded
            deactivate NotificationService
        end

        ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Meeting Role updated
        deactivate ParticipantService
        ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Update successful
        deactivate ParticipantController
        MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG100 - Meeting role updated successfully.
    end
end

deactivate MeetingPage
