title UC-28c - Remove Meeting Participant

actor "Secretary / Chair" as Actor
boundary "Participant Management Page" as ParticipantPage
control ":Participant Controller" as ParticipantController
control ":Participant Service" as ParticipantService
participant ":Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->ParticipantPage: Remove meeting participant
activate ParticipantPage

ParticipantPage->ParticipantController: Remove participant (meetingId, participantId)
activate ParticipantController

ParticipantController->ParticipantService: removeParticipant(meetingId, participantId, actor)
activate ParticipantService

ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Find participant and verify actor
activate ParticipantRepository
ParticipantRepository->Database: Retrieve participant and meeting
activate Database
Database-->ParticipantRepository: Participant and meeting
deactivate Database
ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Participant and meeting
deactivate ParticipantRepository

alt Removal is no longer permitted - EX-1
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: MSG98 - The meeting participant list cannot be modified in its current status.
    ParticipantController-->ParticipantPage: Return status error
    ParticipantPage-->Actor: MSG98 - The meeting participant list cannot be modified in its current status.

else Removal would violate a required meeting role - EX-2
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: MSG109 - The meeting must retain its required Chair and Secretary. Assign a replacement first.
    ParticipantController-->ParticipantPage: Return role error
    ParticipantPage-->Actor: MSG109 - The meeting must retain its required Chair and Secretary. Assign a replacement first.

else Actor is not allowed to manage participants - EX-3
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: MSG85 - You do not have permission to perform this action.
    ParticipantController-->ParticipantPage: Return permission error
    ParticipantPage-->Actor: MSG85 - You do not have permission to perform this action.

else Eligible
    ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Remove participant and retain history (BR-97, BR-100-BR-103)
    activate ParticipantRepository
    ParticipantRepository->Database: Revoke participant
    activate Database
    Database-->ParticipantRepository: Removed
    deactivate Database
    ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Removed
    deactivate ParticipantRepository

    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Participant removed
    ParticipantController-->ParticipantPage: MSG103 - Meeting participant removed successfully.
    ParticipantPage-->Actor: MSG103 - Meeting participant removed successfully.
end

deactivate ParticipantService
deactivate ParticipantController
deactivate ParticipantPage
EOF
