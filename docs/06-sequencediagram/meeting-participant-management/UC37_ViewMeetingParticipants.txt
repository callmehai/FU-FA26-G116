title UC-37 - View Meeting Participants

actor "Secretary" as Secretary
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Participant Controller" as ParticipantController
control ":Meeting Participant Service" as ParticipantService
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Secretary->MeetingPage: Request Meeting Participant list
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->ParticipantController: View Meeting Participants
activate ParticipantController
ParticipantController->ParticipantService: getMeetingParticipants(meetingId, secretaryId)
activate ParticipantService

ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Get current participants and verify assigned Secretary\n(BR-78, BR-84, BR-88)
activate ParticipantRepository
ParticipantRepository->Database: Retrieve Secretary assignment and current participants;\nexclude historical records
activate Database
Database-->ParticipantRepository: Participants / Not accessible / Retrieval failed
deactivate Database
ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Participants / Not accessible / Retrieval failed
deactivate ParticipantRepository

alt Meeting does not exist or is not accessible - EX-1
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Meeting not found
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Meeting not found
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: Meeting not found; no participant data disclosed
else Participant list cannot be retrieved - EX-2
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Participant retrieval failed
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Participant retrieval failed
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG95 - Unable to load meeting participants. Please try again.
else Participant list is retrieved
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Current participant list, possibly empty
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Display Full Name, Email, and Meeting Role
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: Current Meeting Participant list
end

deactivate MeetingPage
