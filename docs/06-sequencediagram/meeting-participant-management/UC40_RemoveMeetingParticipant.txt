title UC-40 - Remove Meeting Participant

actor "Secretary" as Secretary
boundary "Meeting Management Page" as MeetingPage
control ":Meeting Participant Controller" as ParticipantController
control ":Meeting Participant Service" as ParticipantService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Meeting Participant Repository" as ParticipantRepository
participant ":Notification Service" as NotificationService
database ":Database" as Database

Secretary->MeetingPage: Confirm Remove Meeting Participant
activate MeetingPage
MeetingPage->ParticipantController: Remove Meeting Participant
activate ParticipantController
ParticipantController->ParticipantService: removeMeetingParticipant(meetingId, participantId)
activate ParticipantService

ParticipantService->MeetingRepository: Get meeting and current status
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and status
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ParticipantService: Meeting and status
deactivate MeetingRepository

ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Get Secretary assignment, target participant,\nrequired-role counts, and delegation relationships (BR-78, BR-84, BR-89)
activate ParticipantRepository
ParticipantRepository->Database: Retrieve current participant and delegation data
activate Database
Database-->ParticipantRepository: Participant, role counts, and delegations
deactivate Database
ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Participant, role counts, and delegations
deactivate ParticipantRepository

ParticipantService->ParticipantService: Validate removal eligibility and required-role protection\n(BR-78, BR-84, BR-89)

alt Meeting is Ended, MinutesIssued, or Archived - EX-2
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Participant list is read-only
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Removal rejected
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG98 - The meeting participant list can no longer be modified.
else Removal would remove the last required-role holder - EX-1
    ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Required Meeting Role would be removed
    deactivate ParticipantService
    ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Removal rejected
    deactivate ParticipantController
    MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG109 - The meeting must retain its required Chair and Secretary.\nAssign a replacement first.
else Participant can be removed
    ParticipantService->ParticipantRepository: Atomically remove current assignment and end outgoing delegation;\nretain inactive response and attendance evidence when required (BR-84, BR-89)
    activate ParticipantRepository
    ParticipantRepository->Database: Revalidate role counts and save removal
    activate Database
    Database-->ParticipantRepository: Removal saved with affected users / Save failed
    deactivate Database
    ParticipantRepository-->ParticipantService: Removed assignment and affected users / Save failed
    deactivate ParticipantRepository

    alt Participant cannot be removed - EX-3
        ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Save failed
        deactivate ParticipantService
        ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Removal failed
        deactivate ParticipantController
        MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG104 - Unable to remove the meeting participant. Please try again.
    else Participant is removed
        opt Meeting is InProgress
            ParticipantService->ParticipantService: Re-evaluate affected room access; eject only users\nwith no effective room-entry relationship (BR-84)
        end

        opt Meeting is Published or InProgress
            ParticipantService->NotificationService: Queue removal notification without protected link and email;\nrecord delivery failure for retry without rollback (BR-82)
            activate NotificationService
            NotificationService-->ParticipantService: Queued / Retry recorded
            deactivate NotificationService
        end

        ParticipantService-->ParticipantController: Participant removed
        deactivate ParticipantService
        ParticipantController-->MeetingPage: Removal successful
        deactivate ParticipantController
        MeetingPage-->Secretary: MSG103 - Meeting participant removed successfully.
    end
end

deactivate MeetingPage
