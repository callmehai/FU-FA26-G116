title UC-26 - View Meeting Agenda

actor "User" as User
boundary "Meeting Agenda Page" as AgendaPage
control ":Agenda Controller" as AgendaController
control ":Agenda Service" as AgendaService
participant ":Agenda Repository" as AgendaRepository
database ":Database" as Database

User->AgendaPage: Open meeting agenda
activate AgendaPage

AgendaPage->AgendaController: Get agenda (meetingId)
activate AgendaController

AgendaController->AgendaService: getAgenda(meetingId, actor)
activate AgendaService

AgendaService->AgendaRepository: Find meeting and check access
activate AgendaRepository
AgendaRepository->Database: Retrieve agenda and access
activate Database
Database-->AgendaRepository: Agenda / not found
deactivate Database
AgendaRepository-->AgendaService: Agenda / not found
deactivate AgendaRepository

alt Meeting not accessible - EX-1
    AgendaService-->AgendaController: Meeting not accessible
    AgendaController-->AgendaPage: Return not found
    AgendaPage-->User: Meeting not accessible

else Accessible
    AgendaService-->AgendaController: Agenda
    AgendaController-->AgendaPage: Agenda
    AgendaPage-->User: Show agenda (UC-27)
end

deactivate AgendaService
deactivate AgendaController
deactivate AgendaPage
