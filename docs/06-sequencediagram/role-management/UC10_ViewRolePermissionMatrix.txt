title UC-08 - View Role & Permission Matrix

actor "System Admin" as Admin
boundary "Role & Permission Management Page" as RolePage
control ":Role Permission Controller" as RoleController
control ":Role Permission Service" as RoleService
participant ":Role Permission Repository" as RoleRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Admin->RolePage: Access Role & Permission Management
activate RolePage
RolePage->RoleController: Request role and permission matrix
activate RoleController
RoleController->RoleService: Get role and permission matrix
activate RoleService
RoleService->RoleRepository: Get role and permission matrix
activate RoleRepository
RoleRepository->Database: Retrieve role and permission matrix
activate Database
Database-->RoleRepository: Role and permission matrix
RoleRepository-->RoleService: Role and permission matrix
RoleService-->RoleController: Role and permission matrix
RoleController-->RolePage: Display role and permission matrix
RolePage-->Admin: Show role and permission matrix
deactivate Database
deactivate RoleRepository
deactivate RoleService
deactivate RoleController
deactivate RolePage
