title UC-46c - Exclude or Restore a Sentence

actor "Chair / Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Recordings Tab" as RecordingsTab
control ":Speech Clip Controller" as ClipController
control ":Speech Clip Service" as ClipService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecordingsTab: Confirm Exclude with a reason (Overheard or Other),\nor Restore an excluded sentence - AF-1
activate RecordingsTab
RecordingsTab->ClipController: Set the sentence exclusion
activate ClipController
ClipController->ClipService: setExclusion(clipId, actorId, excluded, reason)
activate ClipService
ClipService->ClipService: When excluding, check the reason is Overheard or Other (BR-357)

alt Exclusion reason is invalid - EX-2
    ClipService-->ClipController: MSG354 - Invalid sentence exclusion reason.
    ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG354 - Invalid sentence exclusion reason.
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG354 - Invalid sentence exclusion reason.
else Reason is valid, or restoring
    ClipService->ClipRepository: Get the sentence
    activate ClipRepository
    ClipRepository->Database: Retrieve sentence
    activate Database
    Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence / Not found
    deactivate Database
    ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentence / Not found
    deactivate ClipRepository
    ClipService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting with its current roster
    activate MeetingRepository
    MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and current roster
    activate Database
    Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
    deactivate Database
    MeetingRepository-->ClipService: Meeting and roster
    deactivate MeetingRepository
    ClipService->ClipService: Check the actor is the current Chair or Secretary (BR-357)

    alt Actor is not the current Chair or Secretary - EX-1
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence from the meeting record.
        ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence from the meeting record.
        RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence from the meeting record.
    else Actor may change the exclusion
        ClipService->AuditService: Record the exclusion or restoration with its reason
        activate AuditService
        AuditService-->ClipService: Entry saved
        deactivate AuditService
        ClipService->ClipRepository: Excluding: keep the sentence and save the reason, actor and time.\nRestoring: clear them. Nothing is deleted (BR-357)
        activate ClipRepository
        ClipRepository->Database: Update sentence
        activate Database
        Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence saved / Save failed
        deactivate Database
        ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentence saved / Save failed
        deactivate ClipRepository

        alt Exclusion cannot be saved
            ClipService-->ClipController: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
            ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
            RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG304 - Unable to save transcript changes. Please try again.
        else Exclusion saved
            ClipService->Publisher: Tell everyone in the Meeting that the speech timeline changed
            activate Publisher
            Publisher-->ClipService: Change published
            deactivate Publisher
            alt Sentence restored - AF-1
                ClipService-->ClipController: MSG352 - Sentence restored to meeting record.
                ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG352 - Sentence restored to meeting record.
                RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG352 - Sentence restored to meeting record.
            else Sentence excluded
                ClipService-->ClipController: MSG351 - Sentence excluded from meeting record.
                ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG351 - Sentence excluded from meeting record.
                RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG351 - Sentence excluded from meeting record.
            end
        end
    end
end

deactivate ClipService
deactivate ClipController
deactivate RecordingsTab
