title UC-81a - View My Voice ID

actor "Internal User" as Actor
boundary "My Voice Page" as VoicePage
control ":Voice ID Controller" as VoiceController
control ":Voice ID Service" as VoiceService
participant ":Voice ID Repository" as VoiceRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->VoicePage: Open My voice from the sidebar
activate VoicePage
VoicePage->VoiceController: Get the own Voice ID
activate VoiceController
VoiceController->VoiceService: get(actorId)
activate VoiceService
VoiceService->VoiceRepository: Get the actor's standing consent and voice samples, never anyone else's
activate VoiceRepository
VoiceRepository->Database: Retrieve consent not withdrawn and samples of the actor
activate Database
Database-->VoiceRepository: Consent and samples
deactivate Database
VoiceRepository-->VoiceService: Consent and samples
deactivate VoiceRepository

    VoiceService-->VoiceController: Consent with its notice version, the samples the actor recorded\nwith length, date and whether playable, at most 5 self-recorded samples, the current notice,\nits purpose and the data controller (BR-362, BR-363, BR-448)
    VoiceController-->VoicePage: Consent with its notice version, the samples the actor recorded\nwith length, date and whether playable, at most 5 self-recorded samples, the current notice,\nits purpose and the data controller (BR-362, BR-363, BR-448)
    alt No consent
        VoicePage-->Actor: Show the Voice ID notice with an unticked consent box (UC-81b)
    else Consent given to an older notice version - AF-1
        VoicePage-->Actor: Show the samples and Accept new terms for notice 1.3 (UC-81b, BR-362, BR-449)
    else Consent given to the current notice version
        VoicePage-->Actor: Show the consent, the samples the actor recorded and Record sample\nwhile fewer than 5 self-recorded samples (BR-363, BR-448)
    end
    alt Actor opens the Recorded by Secretary tab, or opens My voice from the notification - AF-2
        VoicePage-->Actor: Show the samples Secretaries recorded for the actor on a meeting room microphone,\nwith Meeting, collector, length, date and used count out of 5; no recording in this tab (BR-448, BR-452)
    end
end

deactivate VoiceService
deactivate VoiceController
deactivate VoicePage
