title UC-55c - Exclude or Restore a Sentence

actor "Chair / Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Meeting Recordings Tab" as RecordingsTab
control ":Speech Clip Controller" as ClipController
control ":Speech Clip Service" as ClipService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Speech Clip Repository" as ClipRepository
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
participant ":Speech Change Publisher" as Publisher
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->RecordingsTab: Confirm Exclude with a reason, or Restore - AF-1
activate RecordingsTab

RecordingsTab->ClipController: Set the sentence exclusion
activate ClipController

ClipController->ClipService: setExclusion(clipId, actorId, excluded, reason)
activate ClipService

ClipService->ClipRepository: Get the sentence
activate ClipRepository
ClipRepository->Database: Retrieve sentence
activate Database
Database-->ClipRepository: Sentence
deactivate Database
ClipRepository-->ClipService: Sentence
deactivate ClipRepository

ClipService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting and its current roster
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting and roster
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting and roster
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->ClipService: Meeting and roster
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt Actor is not the current Chair or Secretary - EX-1
    ClipService-->ClipController: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence.
    ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence.
    RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG353 - Only the Chair or Secretary may exclude a sentence.
else Actor may change the exclusion
    ClipService->AuditService: Record the exclusion or restoration
    activate AuditService
    AuditService-->ClipService: Entry saved
    deactivate AuditService

    ClipService->ClipRepository: Exclude or restore the sentence
    activate ClipRepository
    ClipRepository->Database: Update sentence
    activate Database
    Database-->ClipRepository: Saved
    deactivate Database
    ClipRepository-->ClipService: Saved
    deactivate ClipRepository

    ClipService->Publisher: Tell everyone the timeline changed
    activate Publisher
    Publisher-->ClipService: Published
    deactivate Publisher

    alt Sentence restored - AF-1
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG352 - Sentence restored.
        ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG352 - Sentence restored.
        RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG352 - Sentence restored.
    else Sentence excluded
        ClipService-->ClipController: MSG351 - Sentence excluded.
        ClipController-->RecordingsTab: MSG351 - Sentence excluded.
        RecordingsTab-->Actor: MSG351 - Sentence excluded.
    end
end

deactivate ClipService
deactivate ClipController
deactivate RecordingsTab
