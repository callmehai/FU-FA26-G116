title UC-81c - Record Voice Sample

actor "Internal User" as Actor
boundary "My Voice Page" as VoicePage
control ":Voice ID Controller" as VoiceController
control ":Voice ID Service" as VoiceService
participant ":Voice ID Repository" as VoiceRepository
participant ":Speech Recognition Service" as SpeechEngine
participant ":Voiceprint Protector" as Protector
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->VoicePage: Stop recording the sample passage and save it
activate VoicePage
VoicePage->VoiceController: Save the voice sample
activate VoiceController
VoiceController->VoiceController: Read the form: one audio file of at most 10 MB in a supported format

alt No audio file, or the form is unreadable - EX-6
    VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG313 - Invalid submission data.
    VoicePage-->Actor: MSG313 - Invalid submission data.
else Audio format is not supported - EX-2
    VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG292 - Unsupported audio format. Use MP3, M4A, WAV, WebM, or OGG.
    VoicePage-->Actor: MSG292 - Unsupported audio format. Use MP3, M4A, WAV, WebM, or OGG.
else Audio received
    VoiceController->VoiceService: addSample(actorId, audio, contentType)
    activate VoiceService
    VoiceService->VoiceRepository: Get the actor's standing consent and samples
    activate VoiceRepository
    VoiceRepository->Database: Retrieve consent not withdrawn and samples
    activate Database
    Database-->VoiceRepository: Consent and samples
    deactivate Database
    VoiceRepository-->VoiceService: Consent and samples
    deactivate VoiceRepository
    VoiceService->VoiceService: Check consent is given (BR-361) and fewer than 5 samples are kept (BR-363)

    alt No standing consent - EX-1
        VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG308 - Voice identification consent is required before recording a voice sample.
        VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG308 - Voice identification consent is required before recording a voice sample.
        VoicePage-->Actor: MSG308 - Voice identification consent is required before recording a voice sample.
    else Five samples already kept - EX-4
        VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG310 - Maximum of 5 voice samples reached. Delete an existing sample before recording a new one.
        VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG310 - Maximum of 5 voice samples reached. Delete an existing sample before recording a new one.
        VoicePage-->Actor: MSG310 - Maximum of 5 voice samples reached. Delete an existing sample before recording a new one.
    else Sample may be added
        VoiceService->SpeechEngine: Check the sample and make its voiceprint inside the system (BR-369)
        activate SpeechEngine
        SpeechEngine-->VoiceService: Voiceprint with speech length / Sample rejected with a reason / Unavailable
        deactivate SpeechEngine

        alt Sample fails the quality rules - 6 to 60 seconds of clear speech by one person (BR-363) - EX-3
            VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG309 - Voice sample did not meet quality requirements. Please read the full sample text clearly.
            VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG309 - Voice sample did not meet quality requirements. Please read the full sample text clearly.
            VoicePage-->Actor: MSG309 - Voice sample did not meet quality requirements. Please read the full sample text clearly.
        else Speech Recognition Service unavailable - EX-5
            VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
            VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
            VoicePage-->Actor: MSG312 - Speech recognition service is currently unavailable. Please try again later.
        else Voiceprint made
            VoiceService->VoiceRepository: Lock the consent and recheck it and the 5-sample limit
            activate VoiceRepository
            VoiceRepository->Database: Retrieve consent for update and samples
            activate Database
            Database-->VoiceRepository: Consent and samples
            deactivate Database
            VoiceRepository-->VoiceService: Consent and samples
            deactivate VoiceRepository
            VoiceService->Protector: Protect the voiceprint, bound to the owner, the sample and its model (BR-364)
            activate Protector
            Protector-->VoiceService: Protected voiceprint
            deactivate Protector
            opt Consent is to a notice version that keeps the sample recording
                VoiceService->Protector: Protect the sample recording for its owner to hear (BR-364)
                activate Protector
                Protector-->VoiceService: Protected recording
                deactivate Protector
            end
            VoiceService->AuditService: Record the new sample with its model and speech length
            activate AuditService
            AuditService-->VoiceService: Entry saved
            deactivate AuditService
            VoiceService->VoiceRepository: Save the sample under the consent
            activate VoiceRepository
            VoiceRepository->Database: Insert voice sample
            activate Database
            Database-->VoiceRepository: Sample saved / Save failed
            deactivate Database
            VoiceRepository-->VoiceService: Sample saved / Save failed
            deactivate VoiceRepository

            alt Sample cannot be saved - EX-7
                VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG315 - Unable to save changes. Please try again.
                VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG315 - Unable to save changes. Please try again.
                VoicePage-->Actor: MSG315 - Unable to save changes. Please try again.
            else Sample saved
                VoiceService-->VoiceController: MSG307 - Voice sample saved.
                VoiceController-->VoicePage: MSG307 - Voice sample saved.
                VoicePage-->Actor: MSG307 - Voice sample saved.
            end
        end
    end
    deactivate VoiceService
end

deactivate VoiceController
deactivate VoicePage
