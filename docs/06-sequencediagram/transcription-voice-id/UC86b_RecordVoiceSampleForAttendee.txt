title UC-86b - Record Voice Sample for Attendee

actor "VoiceCollector" as Collector
boundary "Users Page" as UsersPage
control ":Voice Collection Controller" as CollectionController
control ":Voice Collection Service" as CollectionService
participant ":Voice ID Repository" as VoiceRepository
participant ":Speech Recognition Service" as SpeechEngine
participant ":Voiceprint Protector" as Protector
participant ":Audit Service" as AuditService
database ":Database" as Database

Collector->UsersPage: Save the previewed sample for the person
activate UsersPage

UsersPage->CollectionController: Save the voice sample for the person
activate CollectionController

CollectionController->CollectionService: add(userId, actorId, audio, contentType, personAgreed)
activate CollectionService

CollectionService->VoiceRepository: Get the person's consent and collected samples
activate VoiceRepository
VoiceRepository->Database: Retrieve consent and collected samples
activate Database
Database-->VoiceRepository: Consent and collected samples
deactivate Database
VoiceRepository-->CollectionService: Consent and collected samples
deactivate VoiceRepository

alt Person has not agreed under the current terms - EX-1
    CollectionService-->CollectionController: MSG475 - The person has not agreed to Voice ID under the current terms.
    CollectionController-->UsersPage: MSG475 - The person has not agreed to Voice ID under the current terms.
    UsersPage-->Collector: MSG475 - The person has not agreed to Voice ID under the current terms.
else Person already has 50 collected samples - EX-2
    CollectionService-->CollectionController: MSG476 - This person already has 50 collected voice samples.
    CollectionController-->UsersPage: MSG476 - This person already has 50 collected voice samples.
    UsersPage-->Collector: MSG476 - This person already has 50 collected voice samples.
else Samples may be added
    CollectionService->SpeechEngine: Check the sample and make its voiceprint
    activate SpeechEngine
    SpeechEngine-->CollectionService: Voiceprint / Sample rejected
    deactivate SpeechEngine

    alt Audio is unsupported or fails the quality rules - EX-3
        CollectionService-->CollectionController: MSG292 - Unsupported audio format.
        CollectionController-->UsersPage: MSG292 - Unsupported audio format.
        UsersPage-->Collector: MSG292 - Unsupported audio format.
    else Voiceprint made
        CollectionService->Protector: Protect the voiceprint and the recording
        activate Protector
        Protector-->CollectionService: Protected voiceprint and recording
        deactivate Protector

        CollectionService->VoiceRepository: Save the sample and notify the person
        activate VoiceRepository
        VoiceRepository->Database: Insert voice sample and notification
        activate Database
        Database-->VoiceRepository: Saved
        deactivate Database
        VoiceRepository-->CollectionService: Saved
        deactivate VoiceRepository

        CollectionService->AuditService: Record the new collected sample
        activate AuditService
        AuditService-->CollectionService: Entry saved
        deactivate AuditService

        CollectionService-->CollectionController: MSG473 - Voice sample recorded for the attendee.
        CollectionController-->UsersPage: MSG473 - Voice sample recorded for the attendee.
        UsersPage-->Collector: MSG473 - Voice sample recorded for the attendee.
    end
end

deactivate CollectionService
deactivate CollectionController
deactivate UsersPage
