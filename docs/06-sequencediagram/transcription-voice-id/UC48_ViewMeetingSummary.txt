title UC-48 - View Meeting Summary

actor "Chair / Secretary / Participant" as Actor
boundary "Draft Card (Biên bản tab)" as DraftCard
control ":Summary Controller" as SummaryController
control ":Summary Service" as SummaryService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->DraftCard: Open the Biên bản tab
activate DraftCard
DraftCard->SummaryController: get(meetingId)
activate SummaryController
SummaryController->SummaryService: getAsync(meetingId, actorId)
activate SummaryService
SummaryService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting / Not found
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->SummaryService: Meeting / Not found
deactivate MeetingRepository

alt The Meeting is not accessible - EX-2
    SummaryService-->SummaryController: Not found
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: 404
    DraftCard-->Actor: The Meeting is not available
else The actor may read the Meeting
    SummaryService->Database: Retrieve the draft
    activate Database
    Database-->SummaryService: Draft / None
    deactivate Database
    SummaryService->SummaryService: Check whether the actor is the current Chair or Secretary (BR-374)
    alt Actor is the current Chair or Secretary
        SummaryService-->SummaryController: 200 SummaryState with the draft and readiness
    else Draft reviewed
        SummaryService-->SummaryController: 200 SummaryState with the draft, without readiness
    else Draft not reviewed
        SummaryService-->SummaryController: 200 SummaryState with no draft (AF-1)
    end
end
deactivate SummaryService
SummaryController-->DraftCard: 200 SummaryState / 404
deactivate SummaryController

alt Draft shown
    DraftCard-->Actor: Label "Nội dung do AI tạo - cần rà soát", sections I-III, model, time, who asked
    Actor->DraftCard: Read the review state and the source of each part of section II (BR-380)
    DraftCard-->Actor: Who reviewed and when, who corrected and when, the stretch of the Meeting each part came from
else No draft yet - AF-1
    DraftCard-->Actor: Empty state; for the Chair and Secretary, what a draft would be based on (BR-379)
else The last draft failed - AF-2
    DraftCard-->Actor: The reason, and the control to ask again (UC-47)
else Read by another participant - AF-3
    DraftCard-->Actor: The approved draft, read-only, with no correction or review control
end

opt The draft cannot be read - EX-1
    SummaryService-->SummaryController: MSG392 - Unable to load the draft.
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: MSG392 - Unable to load the draft.
    DraftCard-->Actor: MSG392 - Unable to load the draft.
end
deactivate DraftCard
