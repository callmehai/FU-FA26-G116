title UC-50 - Approve Meeting Summary

actor "Chair / Secretary" as Actor
boundary "Draft Card (Biên bản tab)" as DraftCard
control ":Summary Controller" as SummaryController
control ":Summary Service" as SummaryService
participant ":Meeting Repository" as MeetingRepository
participant ":Meeting Summary Repository" as MeetingSummaryRepository
database ":Database" as Database

Actor->DraftCard: Select Approve on a finished draft
activate DraftCard

DraftCard->SummaryController: review(meetingId)
activate SummaryController

SummaryController->SummaryService: reviewAsync(meetingId, actorId)
activate SummaryService

SummaryService->MeetingRepository: Get the Meeting
activate MeetingRepository
MeetingRepository->Database: Retrieve Meeting
activate Database
Database-->MeetingRepository: Meeting
deactivate Database
MeetingRepository-->SummaryService: Meeting
deactivate MeetingRepository

SummaryService->MeetingSummaryRepository: Get the draft
activate MeetingSummaryRepository
MeetingSummaryRepository->Database: Retrieve the draft
activate Database
Database-->MeetingSummaryRepository: Draft / None
deactivate Database
MeetingSummaryRepository-->SummaryService: Draft / None
deactivate MeetingSummaryRepository

alt Actor is not the current Chair or Secretary - EX-1
    SummaryService-->SummaryController: MSG385 - Only the Chair or Secretary may review the draft.
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: MSG385 - Only the Chair or Secretary may review the draft.
    DraftCard-->Actor: MSG385 - Only the Chair or Secretary may review the draft.
else No finished draft - EX-2
    SummaryService-->SummaryController: MSG390 - There is no draft to correct or review.
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: MSG390 - There is no draft to correct or review.
    DraftCard-->Actor: MSG390 - There is no draft to correct or review.
else The AI is writing the draft again - EX-3
    SummaryService-->SummaryController: MSG387 - The AI is writing the draft, please wait.
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: MSG387 - The AI is writing the draft, please wait.
    DraftCard-->Actor: MSG387 - The AI is writing the draft, please wait.
else Draft exists
    SummaryService->MeetingSummaryRepository: Mark the draft reviewed
    activate MeetingSummaryRepository
    MeetingSummaryRepository->Database: Record the reviewer and the time
    activate Database
    Database-->MeetingSummaryRepository: Saved
    deactivate Database
    MeetingSummaryRepository-->SummaryService: Saved
    deactivate MeetingSummaryRepository

    SummaryService-->SummaryController: State
    SummaryController-->DraftCard: MSG391 - Draft approved.
    DraftCard-->Actor: MSG391 - Draft approved.
end

deactivate SummaryService
deactivate SummaryController
deactivate DraftCard
