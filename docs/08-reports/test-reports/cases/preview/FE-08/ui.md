# Online Room — UI (FE-08, ROOM)

Requirement: Online room over LiveKit for internal users and Guests: join and leave, devices, participants, moderation, chat, floating window, requests to speak and the Secretary's meeting notes.

Precondition: Test build of frontend + backend with LiveKit; Chair, Secretary, Member, Organizer and Admin accounts supplied by QA lead; an InProgress Online meeting with that roster; Chrome or Edge with microphone and camera (fake devices allowed) and a second browser for the two-person cases; a verified Guest (UC-04) with a real mailbox for the Guest cases.
