# Recording — UI (FE-09, REC)

Requirement: Recordings tab and the room: room-mic takes, per-person microphone clips, uploaded audio, playback, download, deleting takes and recordings, and managing one's own recorded speech.

Precondition: Backend and frontend running on the test build, signed-in users supplied by QA lead: Chair, Secretary and Member of a meeting in the needed mode (InPerson, Online, Hybrid) and status; the speech worker running for transcript cases; a microphone for the recording cases; a Guest link for the Guest cases. Recordings are personal data: the consent confirmation, the retention date and erasure are checked where they apply.
