# Hotwords for Gipformer (speech/hotwords.py): project words a TrustCabinet meeting says in English, which the model
# tends to write as Vietnamese syllables ("COMM", "REPOT", "DEPROID", "LÔ GỒ" for commit, report, deploy, local).
# One phrase per line, as the model spells it: it writes English loanwords in English letters ("REPORT", "TEST", "DATA"
# come out as such). A line may end in " :<score>" to boost that phrase more or less than SPEECH_HOTWORDS_SCORE.
#
# Keep it short: each phrase also pulls look-alike speech towards it. Common Vietnamese meeting words (biên bản, dự thảo,
# biểu quyết, tài liệu, cuộc họp…) are not listed: the model already gets them right, and on the room recording of
# 23/11/2026 boosting them only added stray "chủ", "họp", "tài liệu" (README, "Từ khoá ưu tiên"). Add a word only
# after hearing the model miss it, and re-run eval/hotwords_wer.py on a room recording and on VIVOS.
#
# "voice" and "bug" stay listed for whoever says them the English way, but said the Vietnamese way they sound like
# "voi" and "bấc"/"bức", and the model writes those syllables whatever the boost.
